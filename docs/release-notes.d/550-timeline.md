- A case's Timeline now shows its events as source lanes under one toolbar,
  each lane headed by its source's name, with a time gutter, links drawn only
  between events the evidence or a rule relates (reviewed links dashed), and an
  accessible List (#550). Sources share one time axis only when one Readmit
  recording session captured them; every other source keeps its own clock and
  is never lined up against another. Events that cannot be placed in time
  appear under Untimed; with no usable times the list opens in Source order.
  Unresolved links and Gaps appear only when there are some, and filter the
  events. Options chooses link rules, Observed, Message time or Source order,
  the display time zone and coverage, and re-reads the case under exactly those
  versions; a failed Apply keeps your choices. Selecting a link shows From, To,
  its basis (the link rules' name, Recorded or Reviewed) and review state;
  Review records Accept or Reject with a reason, Add link joins two messages by
  name, and Undo decision records a new revision, with History listing
  reviewer, time and reason; this works for the case's own recorded links as
  well as links from link rules, and an ambiguous match is never accepted or
  rejected. Decisions are recorded under the Reviewer set in
  Settings, and the window asks for one only when none is set; it is a local
  name, not a signed-in identity. Link rules and coverage are named objects
  edited and saved whole from the Timeline menu; a coverage window can be
  entered in its own time zone, and a time that zone skips or repeats is
  refused at its field.

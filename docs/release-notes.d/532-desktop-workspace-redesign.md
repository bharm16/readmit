- The desktop application is redesigned. It opens on Projects: your recent projects, New project, Open… and a demo. A sidebar moves between Cases, Tests, Runs, Environments and Reports of the open project, and Tools, Settings and Help. One page is shown at a time, with tabs inside it, and an opened message's details sit beside the page. Licensing, team, runners, security and storage moved into Settings, and privacy and capability statements into Help. The window uses the system's own colours, light or dark, and your accent colour.
- Every panel is rebuilt as labelled fields, tables and one clear action per step. Explanatory paragraphs and internal names are gone from the working screens. The timeline loads when opened and shows problems such as a missing acknowledgement on the rows they affect.
- The run view no longer shows a nonproduction badge before an environment is chosen, and a production or unclassified environment is never described as nonproduction.

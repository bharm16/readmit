# Commercial terms — owner/counsel review draft

**Draft only. Not an operative EULA, privacy notice, signed agreement, offer or
change to existing rights.** D8 adopts the direction below; commercial release
requires owner/counsel approval and the actual seller's identity, contact routes,
applicable jurisdictions and approved order process. Do not present this file as
accepted terms or imply prior distributions have been relicensed.

## Proposed product terms / EULA

Readmit-owned application code is intended to be offered under a proprietary
commercial license. The approved order and signed entitlement would identify the
customer organization, subscription term, purchased author seats and runner
capacity. An author is a named human with up to two active devices. An authorized
administrator may transfer assignments. Runner capacity measures simultaneous
active execution instances, not messages or tests. Read-only reviewers are free.
The customer-hosted hub is included in the adopted annual author/runner offering.
No amount or price is fixed by this draft or by the engine.

Customers retain ownership and control of their evidence and specifications.
The proposed application license grants no vendor ownership or automatic right
to access those materials. Expiry stops new licensed authoring/execution as the
implemented admission policy provides, permits already-started bounded work to
finish and preserves reading, verification and export of existing evidence.
Cancellation stops future renewal without retroactively removing the paid term
or configured licensing grace. An offline copy cannot immediately learn a
revocation; copied stores and restored VM snapshots remain explicit limits.

A final EULA must specify permitted installation/use, assignment, redistribution,
subscription/renewal and cancellation procedure, refund/proration rules consistent
with the approved merchant process, termination, warranties, liability,
disputes/governing law and required statutory exceptions. These are unresolved
legal provisions, not permissions or waivers invented here. Product outputs
are engineering evidence; this draft makes no clinical-safety, regulatory,
HIPAA or other certification guarantee.

Existing third-party licenses and previously granted rights remain intact.
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md), the `licenses/` directory and
applicable component licenses govern their respective components. A final notice
review must cover the exact CLI, desktop and customer-hub artifacts and every
required transitive license/source obligation. This draft neither grants
ownership of third-party work nor replaces its terms.

## Proposed account-data privacy notice

The separate vendor account service would process business contact mailboxes,
organization identifiers, administrator-selected author/device names, runner
allocations, invoice/payment references, renewal state and signed entitlement
history for account administration, payment reconciliation, delivery and support.
Payment credentials and provider invoices belong in the merchant's hosted flow;
they are not members of the local administration snapshot. No automatic raw
clinical evidence, case titles, endpoint values, patient identifiers, evidence
hashes, hardware identifiers or usage telemetry are collected by this package.
Do not put those values into account identifiers or contact fields.

The final notice must name the actual data controller, contact and rights-request
route; purposes and applicable processing grounds; actual merchant/hosting/support
recipients and locations; transfer safeguards; retention/deletion schedules and
legal retention exceptions; security and incident processes; and applicable
individual rights. None are asserted to be operational by this draft. The owner
must approve and implement them before collecting real account data. Local
snapshots require access control and protected backup; deleting one copy cannot
prove deletion from provider records or backups.

## Proposed support terms

Annual maintained-software subscriptions include business-hours support:
**Monday–Friday 09:00–17:00 America/Chicago**, with a **first-response target of
two business days**. This is a response target, not a resolution guarantee.
There is no 24/7 clinical or production incident SLA. The final terms must
publish the actual support route, covered versions, staffing/escalation process
and any holiday rules before claiming an operational service.

Standard support accepts reviewed metadata and synthetic reproductions only.
Customers review all attachments and remain responsible for authorizing disclosure.
There is no automatic upload or default remote access. Support entitlement is
reported for an annual paid term, including a cancelled subscription's remaining
paid period; trial/provisional support and any extension beyond expiry require an
explicit separately approved offering. Licensing grace alone makes no support
promise. Emergency clinical incidents follow the customer's own response process.

## Separate path before offering PHI access

PHI access is not part of standard support and is not offered by this draft.
If the owner proposes such access, stop before receiving it. Owner/counsel and
the customer's authorized privacy/security representatives must review the actual
roles, lawful disclosure basis and appropriate agreements, including whether a
business associate agreement is required. Separately approve minimum necessary
scope, named authorized personnel, access controls, approved transfer/storage,
retention/deletion and incident obligations. Only a completed arrangement can
activate that path; a checkbox, signature on a packet, synthetic test or generic
claim of de-identification cannot substitute. Keep the standard synthetic-only
path available when no such arrangement exists.
